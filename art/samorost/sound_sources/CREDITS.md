# Sound credits

The soundtrack of `art/samorost/animated/` is made by `tools/samorost/animate/sound.py`.
The music box melody, pad, gear ticks, propeller buzz, bubbles, nose drip, the beast's snoring,
fire crackle, firefly sparkles, the owl and the shooting star are synthesised there.
